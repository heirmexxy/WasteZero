## Type
**Inter Display Bold** for headlines and **Inter Regular** for body text. Free and open-licensed: rsms.me/inter (also on Google Fonts).

## Voice
Plain, specific, numbers first. Say what it saves and how we prove it.
- **Say:** "Saved $1,240 this month: 92% of answers identical, checked on your own traffic."
- **Not:** "Revolutionary AI-powered cost optimisation."
- Lead with the customer's pain: *"Our AI bill doubled and nobody can explain why."*, *"An agent looped overnight and cost us $4,000."*
- Never promise a percentage saving before an audit. Offer the free audit instead.
